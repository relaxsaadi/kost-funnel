## Function-specific limitations carried forward

- `TABLEAU 7.8.A` ends at 6.2.5. There is **no official Block 7** for Function 7.8. Course slides 112–116 on accident/incident/undeclared-DG reporting remain over-taught supplementary content and are not assigned invented task IDs here.
- Batch 3 identified genuine course pools at slide 79 (special provisions) and slides 90–91 (lithium battery comparison) that Stage 1 had not assigned to an official 7.8 leaf. This matrix does not manufacture a mapping for them. Any future mapping change must first be justified against the official task semantics and the Stage 1/2A governance chain.
- The local `Cadre CBTA` document self-labelled for Function 7.8 is excluded from evidence because it describes a physical handling/ramp role and cites a different table/framework. It must not be used to fill a gap in this matrix.
- Direct 67th Edition 2026 locators recorded above are evidence leads, not a blanket `SOURCE VERIFIED` promotion. Every regulatory fact in a production item still requires its own current direct evidence and durable FR source-verification provenance.
- FR source verification and EN bilingual review remain independent. `pending` reviewer fields are intentionally non-positive. No `APPROVED` state is present without a named qualified reviewer and ISO review date.
