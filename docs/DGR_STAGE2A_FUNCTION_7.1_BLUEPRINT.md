## Blueprint rule

The historical corrected Stage 2A blueprint was explicitly **source-yield driven**, not quota driven. A ceiling is the maximum number of genuinely distinct, source-supported items then considered defensible for a task. It is not a target to pad toward. Where a historical per-task ceiling was not recovered into the repository, this blueprint records `SOURCE GAP — HISTORICAL CEILING NOT RECOVERED` rather than deriving a number from another function, from the current question count, or by arithmetic from a subtotal.

Current production eligibility remains a later gate: regulatory claims require direct current DGR 67th Edition 2026 Tier A evidence, explicit GAP/CONFLICT handling, named/date FR source verification, separate qualified bilingual EN review, and final approval-chain evidence.
