## Function-specific limitations carried forward

- `TABLEAU 7.10.A` deliberately omits `0.4.2`, `5.1.3`-style extra leaves, `6.2.*`, and Block-7 leaf `7.4`. Local over-taught or assessment-only content must not create unofficial task identities.
- Function 7.10's official title includes passenger/crew baggage, freight and mail, but the KOST course has only thin freight/mail-specific procedural development compared with Function 7.2. The Block 3 rows therefore retain this limitation rather than claiming a dedicated cargo-acceptance workflow that the source does not teach.
- `0.3.2` passenger responsibilities remains a hard local `SOURCE GAP`.
- Direct 67th Edition 2026 locators recorded above are evidence leads, not a blanket `SOURCE VERIFIED` promotion. Every regulatory fact in a production item still requires its own current direct evidence and durable FR source-verification provenance.
- FR source verification and EN bilingual review remain independent. `pending` reviewer fields are intentionally non-positive. No `APPROVED` state is present without a named qualified reviewer and ISO review date.
