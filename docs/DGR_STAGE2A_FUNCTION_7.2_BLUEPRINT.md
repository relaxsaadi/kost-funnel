## Method

For each of the 23 leaf sub-tasks, the count is derived from the "Coverage
assessment" column already recorded in `docs/DGR_STAGE1_FUNCTION_7.2_DRAFT.md`'s
sub-task enumeration tables (itself now cross-validated), converted to a
provisional ceiling using this banding:

| Stage 1 coverage assessment | Ceiling band |
|---|---|
| Very strong (multi-slide, multiple exam+practice hits, worked examples) | 8 |
| Strong (dedicated slide(s), clear exam/practice trace) | 3–4 |
| Moderate-strong | 2–3 |
| Adequate / adequate-but-shallow (pointer-to-reference, no dedicated depth) | 1 |
| Generic-awareness-only (may not invent employer/procedure-specific content) | 1, sample 0–1 |
| SOURCE GAP | 0 |

**Sample** = the recommended maximum number of items drawn from that
sub-task's pool for a single exam sitting, mirroring the "sample" column
`docs/RECOVERED_STAGE2A_CONTEXT.md` records for Function 7.1. It is a
per-sub-task draw ceiling, not a claim that every sub-task must appear in
every sitting — an actual exam-generation policy (e.g. "draw N sub-tasks at
random, 1 item each, to hit a target length") is a platform-configuration
decision outside this blueprint's scope.

## Block 7 — Collecte de données pour la sécurité (★★, all four leaves)

### Combined-pool decision for 7.1/7.2 (accident vs. incident reporting)

The cross-validation pass (see `docs/DGR_STAGE1_FUNCTION_7.2_DRAFT.md`)
found that **every single occurrence** of "accident" and "incident" in the
entire 117-slide course pairs the two words together ("accidents et
incidents" / "accident ou incident"), with zero independently-worded
treatment of either term alone, and zero occurrences of either word in the
exam or practice book. This is not a case of "thin coverage of two real,
distinguishable things" — it is the complete absence of any material that
could support two non-overlapping question pools.

**Decision: 7.1 and 7.2 are treated as ONE combined question pool**,
dual-tagged to both official IATA sub-task codes (a produced item counts
toward satisfying both 7.1 and 7.2's coverage simultaneously, not as two
separate items). Drafting two artificially "different" pools from one
undifferentiated slide would require inventing a distinction the source
does not make — precisely the fabrication this program's rules prohibit.
If future source material (a revised KOST course, or direct DGR 9.6.5 Tier
A text) is found to draw a genuine accident/incident distinction, this
combined-pool decision should be revisited and the pool split at that time.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Binding source restrictions carried forward

1. Every "DGR x.y.z" section number appearing anywhere in the Stage 1 trace
   or to be used in a future question stem is **Tier B** (as displayed on
   the KOST slide), not yet checked against the current IATA DGR 67th
   Edition (French, Addendum 1) text. No item reaches Tier A status without
   that separate verification pass, the same discipline used for
   Q-7.1-001–012.
2. **0.4.3 stays at 0 questions** until new KOST material or direct Tier A
   DGR text is supplied — do not draft against it from inference or from
   Table 2.3.A's incidental "sans danger subsidiaire" phrase (a different
   source document, not Function 7.2 training material).
3. **7.1/7.2 is one combined, dual-tagged pool** (3 items) — do not draft
   two separate sets of 3 items each under the theory that "accident" and
   "incident" must each get their own dedicated pool; the source does not
   support that split.
4. **0.6.2 is generic-awareness only** (matching Function 7.1's identical
   constraint): items may test that personnel must know/follow the
   employer's emergency intervention procedure, never invent
   employer-specific procedural content.
5. **0.2.3 and 0.5.1 are thin/shallow, not gaps** — 1 question each is the
   defensible ceiling; do not inflate to match richer sub-tasks' counts.
6. Every FR item must have its source (slide/page, exam/practice-book
   question number) recorded before drafting, per the KOST source trace
   already logged in `docs/DGR_STAGE1_FUNCTION_7.2_DRAFT.md`.
7. No item reaches `APPROVED` without a named qualified human reviewer and
   review date. This blueprint assigns no such status to anything — it is
   pre-review scaffolding only.
8. FR source verification and EN bilingual technical review remain two
   separate gates, exactly as for Function 7.1; neither is addressed by
   this blueprint.
9. Do not assume this blueprint's counts, bands, or combined-pool pattern
   transfer to Function 7.3 or any later function — each requires its own
   independently-derived Stage 2A blueprint from its own source material,
   per `.claude/rules/dgr-stage2b.md`.
