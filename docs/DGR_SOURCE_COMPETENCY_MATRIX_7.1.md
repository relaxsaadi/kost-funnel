## Function-specific limitations carried forward

- The recovered Stage 1 table is the source of the exact 44 task identities and wording. `0.3.2` remains excluded rather than copied from the generic table.
- `0.2.1` and `1.1.2` share a restricted prohibited-DG pool. Acétylène (liquide) must not be generalized to UN1001 or UN3374.
- `1.2.3` remains fail-closed because the UN1815/UN1816 Propyltrichlorosilane source conflict is unresolved and arbitrary Table 4.2 lookups outside the governed closed pool are not allowed.
- `1.2.4` must preserve approval versus exemption/derogation mechanisms: UN1541/A2 is an approval example; no mechanism may be asserted for UN1098 without current direct evidence.
- `0.6.2` is generic-awareness only. No employer-specific KOST emergency procedure may be invented.
- `2.2.4` is clause-only and must not become an invented assembly sequence.
- `2.4.3` remains labels-only for the current verified item; marks and PSN/UN rules are separate.
- Class 7 content is mapped only where the official task wording fits; its presence does not expand unrelated leaves.
- Direct 67th Edition 2026 locators above are evidence leads, not blanket human `SOURCE VERIFIED` promotions. Every regulatory fact and distractor still requires item-specific traceability.
- FR source verification and EN bilingual review remain independent. The existing Q-7.1-001–012 EN package is a draft based on reconstructed FR working glosses, not completed bilingual review; Q-7.1-013–019 remain outside that package.
- No `APPROVED` state is present without a named qualified reviewer and an ISO review date.
